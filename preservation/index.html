A notice counts as sent only when the outgoing message is on file. Dates are the first notice on file for each institution. "FOI/EIR" marks bodies covered by the Freedom of Information Act 2000 or the Environmental Information Regulations 2004; private bodies receive a request to preserve together with an enquiry about access. Replies: each answer from the body on file to 10 Nov 2026, on any request, not only the notice. “Rejected / refused” covers a refusal, a declined request, an exemption applied, a statement that FOI or EIR does not apply, a refusal to preserve, or a statement that the record asked for is not held; automatic acknowledgements are not counted as replies. A fee quote is listed as “fee quoted, not paid” and is never counted as a refusal. Machine-readable copy: register.json.
