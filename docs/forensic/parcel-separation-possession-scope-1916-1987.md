# Parcel separation, possession scope and title continuity

**Correction baseline:** 25 November 2026  
**Scope:** Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth; 1916–1987  
**Purpose:** Reconcile the public parcel model and prevent later court recitals or composite title descriptions from silently replacing the underlying parcel evidence.

## Controlling distinctions

1. **Frederick Buckler's tenancy:** the corrected family position is that Fred's agricultural tenancy related to **Parcel B only**. It did not extend to Parcel A or create a tenancy for Mary Williams.
2. **Mary Williams's occupation:** Mary occupied and asserted an independent ownership claim to **Parcel A2 (the farmhouse, buildings and garden)**. Her position must not be derived from Fred's Parcel B tenancy. The family position is that there was no tenancy of Parcel A.
3. **Parcel structure:** Parcel A comprises A1 (northern woodland) and A2 (house, buildings and garden). Parcel B is the separate eastern/right-hand agricultural fields. Parcel C is the southern green, outside Parcel A and distinct from Parcel B.
4. **Date-specific “whole farm”:** the 1916 meaning must not be assumed to be the meaning in 1955. The family reconstruction is that a relevant term in the 1877 agreement took effect in 1928 and changed the relevant parcel interests. The original instrument, plan and 1928 evidence must establish the legal effect.
5. **1939 agreement:** the WGR–John Williams agreement and plan are associated with Parcel B only. The family disputes the attributed John Williams signature and says the consideration was not paid. Record these as authenticity, execution and performance issues; do not publish forgery or fraud as established fact.
6. **1949:** Frederick's agricultural tenancy is Parcel B only. Separate attempts in 1949 to make Mary's occupation permissive the family reports that those attempts did not succeed. Those attempts must be examined separately from Fred's tenancy.
7. **1955 order versus execution:** the corrected family reconstruction is that Order 14's legal scope was Parcel B only. The physical taking on 4 July 1955 is said to have comprised Parcel B, Parcel C and the northern woodland (A1). The farmhouse and garden (A2) were not taken; Mary remained there asserting ownership. Do not equate the order's words “whole farm” with the land physically entered or taken.
8. **1962 proceedings:** the family case is that the 1962 possession order relied on ambiguity in the 1955 order and concerned a different parcel from the 1939 Parcel B agreement. The original 1962 pleadings, order, plan, evidence and mesne-profits schedule must be examined before this is stated as a proven legal defect.
9. **1982–87 registration:** WA231076 (1982) and WA240304 (1983) must be analysed as separate first registrations. The family disputes whether the 1982 title established the farmhouse parcel registered in 1983, whether Mary received proper notice or an opportunity to object, and the significance of the 2 February 1987 amalgamation. The complete files and plans are required.

## Publication rules

- Never describe Frederick as tenant of Parcel A or A1.
- Never imply the 1939 plan includes the farmhouse and garden.
- Never state that the 1939 agreement was forged, fraudulent or paid unless the original and corroborating records establish the relevant fact.
- Never state that the 1955 order included all land physically taken. Publish separate fields for **order scope** and **execution footprint**.
- Never describe the 1962 order as conclusively invalid on the basis of a later recital. Inspect the original proceedings.
- Never merge WA231076 and WA240304 retrospectively when describing what was registered or before the Court of Appeal in 1987.
- Distinguish **documented**, **family account**, **working interpretation**, **allegation**, **disputed**, and **not yet verified** on every page.
- Preserve superseded wording in the correction history; do not silently erase the development of the research.
