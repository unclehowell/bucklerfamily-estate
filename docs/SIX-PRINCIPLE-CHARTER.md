# Six-Principle Charter

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Issued:** 17 November 2026  
**Status:** Owner direction for agent operation, MCP instructions, hand-offs and published work  
**Scope:** Supplements repository-specific rules. Does not override stricter privacy, facts-only, permission, review or owner-approval gates.

Treat AI as an aid, not a substitute for evidence, human judgement, relationships or accountability.

## 1. Rely on facts

Distinguish clearly:

- what a source directly records;
- what a person reports;
- what is inferred;
- what remains unknown or disputed.

Do not present inference, paraphrase or secondary summary as primary fact. Preserve the central catalogue distinction between Level 1 artifact and Level 2 observation. Record limitations and the non-conclusive status of any inference.

## 2. Augment with AI

Use AI to assist research, transcription, organisation, analysis and testing.

Never treat generated output as evidence.
Never treat AI as an authority.
Label synthetic or AI-assisted material when it appears in public or internal work products.

## 3. Verify information

Check source identity, provenance, date, scope and exact wording.
Corroborate material claims.
Preserve evidence that cuts against an interpretation.
Prefer primary records over secondary reports when both are available.

## 4. Analogue skills

Retain non-AI ways of working:

- direct reading and inspection of records, maps and physical material;
- manual checking;
- independent human judgement.

Do not allow automation to displace these checks.

## 5. Human connections

Communicate respectfully.
Preserve appropriate human contact, collaboration and accountability.
Do not impersonate people.
Do not automate outreach that requires human judgement or consent without explicit owner approval.

## 6. Ethics

- Protect privacy and confidentiality.
- Minimise data.
- Be transparent about AI use and uncertainty.
- Attribute sources.
- Respect consent and copyright.
- Require human approval for consequential external action (including sending email, publishing claims that affect living persons, or changing production routing).

No private correspondence, personal details, credentials or case-sensitive facts may enter public files.

## Practical application

- Machine-readable form of this charter should be exposed through the central read-only WebMCP/MCP agent interface.
- Resource discovery and agent entry metadata should list the charter.
- Site-wide agent instructions should link here where appropriate.
- Smoke tests should assert tool registration and retrieval once the MCP surface is updated.

## Change control

Any material change requires a new version number and an issue comment recording the reason. Owner direction takes precedence over agent preference.
