# Friends of National Museum Wales (Cyfeillion Amgueddfa Cymru) — lecture and Oriel magazine route

Checked 5 Nov 2026 (homepage read directly).

## What the record shows
- Members' charity founded in 1955 (reg. 701990) that supports all seven National Museum Wales sites. Member of the British Association of Friends of Museums.
- Runs "monthly public lectures and Zoom talks on topics of artistic, historical and scientific interest", plus excursions, behind-the-scenes visits and an annual Christmas lunch. Members get cheaper entry to public lectures.
- Magazine *Oriel*, published twice a year and described as award-winning. Past articles are online (indexed up to Nov 2024). Event news goes out three times a year.
- Members get 10% off in all Museum shops and cafés. Gift memberships are promoted.
- Funds go to museum projects (organ recitals at National Museum Cardiff, Slate Museum excavator restoration, Roman Legion Museum Pegasus Centre).
- The events page URL we tried (/events/) returned 404. The current lecture list and how speakers are chosen are **not checked**.

## How to work it
1. Read the latest *Oriel* articles online first, to match length and tone and avoid repeating a topic.
2. Offer one thing: either a lecture or Zoom talk, or a short *Oriel* article (heritage and objects, illustrated). Contact through the address printed on the site ([address kept in the project workspace] is the website contact). Ask for the programme secretary.
3. The audience is museum supporters who join lectures and trips: pitch objects, records and buildings, not argument.
4. Close each talk or article with a QR code to a free sample and the newsletter (utm_source=friendsmuseumwales, utm_medium=talk|magazine). Never sell at the lecture.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- Topic in our voice: "Tŷ Mawr (Great House): reading a Glamorgan manorial house and former court house through its records". The angle is its standing at its height. "Farm" appears only in quoted record titles.
- Caution: we have open information-request correspondence with Museum Wales (FOI 2026-020). Keep any Friends approach entirely separate from it, and neutral. Don't mention requests, refusals or the case. The Friends are a separate charity from the museum.
- Priority: MEDIUM. A small but well-matched heritage audience, and *Oriel* lasts longer than one talk. Outreach: draft it as a HubSpot task only, on the Executive's GO; never send.
