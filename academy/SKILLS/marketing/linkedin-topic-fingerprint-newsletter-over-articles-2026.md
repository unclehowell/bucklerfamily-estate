# LinkedIn 2026: topic fingerprinting, comment system, and newsletters over standalone articles (van der Blom, 1.3M posts)

Date: 2026-11-04 · Topic: LinkedIn growth / newsletter / community · Sources at foot.

## The evidence (practitioner testimony + dataset)
Richard van der Blom (author of the annual LinkedIn Algorithm Insights report) on Creator Science ep. 307, 2 Jun 2026; transcript read:
- Dataset: **1.3M posts from 50,000 creators** for the 2026 report.
- **Reach down ~60%** for active creators over two years; LinkedIn shifted from a relationship graph to an **interest-based graph** ("biggest change in 10 years").
- **Topic fingerprinting**: LinkedIn learns who you are first from your **profile**, then from what you post and what you comment on. He rewrote his own profile in Feb 2026 (first time in 3 years), removing old resume material, aimed at his ideal reader. Commenting on off-topic posts "actively hurts your reach".
- About 80% of the 20-30 comments he gets in the first five minutes are AI-written (his own count; one account).
- **Comment system: 25-30 min/day**: a check ~15 min at lunchtime, then an end-of-day pass through his lists.
- **Bookmark search** (free curated feed): Search > Posts > filter by the people you never want to miss (e.g. 12 names) > sort by recent / date range; save the search URL as a bookmark and use it daily.
- **Standalone articles ~0.4x normal reach**; the same long-form written as a **newsletter edition** is "way up" on reach, engagement and conversion. He sends one every Sunday since Jan 2026, 130,000 subscribers.
- New followers are prompted in their inbox to subscribe to your newsletter; **on his account ~60% of new followers subscribe** (his account only, not generalisable).
- Newsletter analytics now show **clicks per link** (he runs 6-7 CTAs per edition).
- Optimal frequency fell from **5-6 to 2-4 posts per week**; text-only needs exceptional copy.
- Trade data, same period: van der Blom LinkedIn post. Creators 100k+ followers reach -18% since Mar 2026, 5k-10k followers +4% (snippet, not read in full).

## How to do it (checklist)
1. Profile first: headline + About + Featured all on ONE topic (the reader you want). Remove unrelated history. Re-check every quarter.
2. Pick 2-3 topic words and use them in every post and comment; no off-topic commenting from the posting profile.
3. Build one bookmark search of 10-15 relevant accounts; spend 25-30 min/day in it with substantive, human-written comments. No pods, no automated comments (already indexed policy skill: linkedin-pods-automated-comments-policy-2026.md).
4. Launch a LinkedIn **newsletter** (not standalone articles); fixed day (e.g. Sunday), one long-form edition, 2-4 short posts in between that point to it.
5. Track per edition: subscribers gained, clicks per link (new analytics), followers->subscribers ratio.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- Posting profile = the saved "LinkedIn — Cedwyn posting" login. Profile rewrite and newsletter creation are a **new public channel**: PROPOSAL to the project workspace, not done by a harvest run.
- Topic fingerprint words: Welsh local history, Llandough/Leckwith heritage, period records. Not the case, not accusations.
- Newsletter idea: weekly "From the records" edition = one verbatim period-record quote + source + one link to the matching Cedwyn page and one to /ebooks/ (2 CTAs, per-link clicks then show what sells). Reuse quote-pack cards so no new art is needed.
- Comments: human only, on heritage/archive posts in the bookmark list; the SOCIAL ENGAGEMENT runner does not cover LinkedIn and must not (automation policy).
- Editorial rules apply: never name the owner publicly, banned words list, no outbound links beyond our own pages, nothing about family holdings.
