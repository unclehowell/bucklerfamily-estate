# Vale of Glamorgan Agricultural Show trade stand (Penllyn): rules, fees and a go/no-go for Cedwyn

Topic: the Vale's biggest one-day show as a local sales/sign-up venue for the farm history. Harvested 2026-11-05. 'vale of glamorgan show' / 'agricultural show' had 0 hits in INDEX.md before (the Royal Welsh Show is covered separately).

## Facts (read on the society's site and its 2026 PDFs, 5 Nov 2026)
- Society's own figures (not checked): 20,000+ visitors, "largest one day event in the Vale", 200+ trade stands, a large Craft Fair, a Shopping Mall "which allows small traders to exhibit", a Countryside Marquee, Home Produce and Horticultural marquees, food court (separate form). Adult ticket £20/day on 2026 posts (Instagram snippet).
- 2026 application form (posted Mar 2026): first come first served; 10% surcharge after 31 May; passes sent in July; pay by BACS before access ("no trader on their pitch without cleared payment").
  - Category C (Commercial companies, enterprises, charities, political parties): minimum £150 for 4 m x 4 m, +£20 per extra metre of frontage; prime ringside +20% by arrangement.
  - A/B/D are machinery, vehicles, vintage vehicles. Craft Fair / Shopping Mall small-trader rates are not on this form (ask the secretary; not checked).
- Regulations: positioned on arrival, no maps in advance; pitch access from 06:00, no vehicles after 08:00, trading by 09:00; NOTHING may leave before 18:00; take all rubbish away; public liability insurance mandatory (evidence on the stand); signed risk assessment returned with the form (PAT-tested electrics, LPG, generators need fire equipment).

## How to judge it (fee-multiple rule)
Use market-stall-book-economics-fee-multiple-madsen.md: a table pays when gross is 5-10x the fee. Category C minimum £150 -> needs £750-1,500 gross on the day. Add the cost of insurance and a full 06:00-18:00 day for a person on the stand.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- Fit is real: an agricultural show audience in the Vale is exactly who remembers farms around Llandough, and the farm-history angle suits the Countryside Marquee.
- Money does not work for e-books alone: at ~£4.99 the £150 minimum needs 150-300 sales. Verdict for 2027: NO own Category C stand.
- Cheaper doors to check before spring 2027 (all PROPOSALS, nothing sent): (1) the Craft Fair / Shopping Mall small-trader rate; (2) whether the Countryside Marquee hosts local-history displays free; (3) sharing a local history/civic society's stand (see civic-society and heritage-talks skills). Goal on the day: sign-ups for the free first edition and walk places, plus print copies once a paperback exists.
- Timeline if the project lead states yes: watch the site from March 2027 for the new form; apply before 31 May to avoid the 10% surcharge; insurance in place before July passes.
- Rules: zero spend without the project lead's yes; booking or emailing the secretary is an outward step in his name (draft only); public text never names him; nothing about family holdings or addresses on stand material; banned words apply to banners/leaflets.
