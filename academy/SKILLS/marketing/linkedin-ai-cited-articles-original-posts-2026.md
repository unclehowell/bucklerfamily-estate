# LinkedIn content that AI search cites: articles 500-2,000 words, original knowledge posts, person + Page (Semrush x LinkedIn, 2026)

Skill harvested 2026-11-04. Topic: LinkedIn growth / AI visibility.

## What the source shows (with numbers)
Semrush, "We Analyzed 89K LinkedIn URLs Cited in AI Search" (Margarita Loktionova, 10 Mar 2026). Method: 325,000 prompts (Jan-Feb 2026) across ChatGPT Search, Google AI Mode and Perplexity, in 12 industries. 89,000 unique LinkedIn URLs were cited, with extra data from LinkedIn.
- **Reach:** LinkedIn was the #2 cited domain, in **11% of AI answers** on average (ChatGPT Search 14.3%, AI Mode 13.5%, Perplexity 5.3%), ahead of Wikipedia, YouTube and the news sites in their set.
- **Echo:** AI answers closely mirror the cited LinkedIn text (semantic similarity **0.57-0.60**, against Reddit 0.53-0.54 and Quora 0.435). So the first lines of a post shape how the topic gets explained.
- **Format:**
  - Articles were **50-66%** of cited LinkedIn content and feed posts 15-28%.
  - The most-cited article length was **500-2,000 words**. The most-cited post length was **50-299 words**.
  - **~95% of cited posts are original**; reshares were about 5%.
  - **54-64%** of cited posts share knowledge or practical advice (almost two-thirds on AI Mode). Promotional posts come second.
- **Author:**
  - Most cited posts had *moderate* engagement (**15-25 reactions**). Virality was not needed.
  - **~75%** of cited authors posted **5+ times in 4 weeks**, and nearly half had over 2,000 followers.
- **Person vs Page:** Perplexity cites Company Pages most (**59%** of its LinkedIn citations). ChatGPT Search and AI Mode cite individual members **59%** of the time.

Caveats: Semrush sells AI-visibility tools and LinkedIn co-supplied data. The prompts lean towards B2B and tech, so heritage and history prompts are likely under-represented. These figures are correlations, not tests.

## How to do it (steps)
1. **Write one LinkedIn article (500-2,000 words) per topic people actually ask about.** Use a question or plain-topic headline, a 2-3 sentence direct answer at the top, dated facts, then the detail.
2. **State the core message in the first lines, in the exact terms you want repeated.** AI paraphrases LinkedIn closely, so vague openers get echoed vaguely.
3. **Make feed posts original and 50-299 words.** Explain one thing, share a first-hand finding, or document a result. Don't count reshares as output.
4. **Keep a steady cadence of 5+ posts per 4 weeks.** Consistency correlates with citation more than reactions do.
5. **Use both a person and a Page.** Individual posts matter for ChatGPT and AI Mode, and a Company Page for Perplexity.
6. **Measure:** add 2-3 LinkedIn-relevant prompts to the AI visibility check and log whether a linkedin.com URL is cited, and whose.
