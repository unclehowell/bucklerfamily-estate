# Consensus first, then something only you can say: earning the AI citation instead of being absorbed (2026)

Harvested 2026-11-04 from Advanced Web Ranking's blog. Figures are the authors' own; small samples; not checked by us.

## Evidence
- **AWR passage-level study (13 Aug 2026):** 40 informational prompts on one day (29 Jul 2026), Google AI Overviews vs Bing Copilot; 265 citations, 112 coded passages, screenshots + coding sheets published.
  - AI Overview appeared on 40/40 queries; cited 16.8 sources on average (3-32). Copilot cited 4.9 (4-7).
  - 59% of AI Overview citations were Reddit (43), YouTube (49) or LinkedIn (27): the engine cites a spread of independent surfaces that agree (corroboration).
  - On 11 of 12 fully captured AIOs every visible organic result was also cited; the exception: the #1 result's definition fed the answer but got no credit because it had **no visible date** and read like five other definitions.
  - Cited (n=95) vs surfaced-but-uncited (n=17) passages: fresh 2025-26 date visible 80% vs 53%; entity named at first mention 96% vs 82%; pure consensus restatement 61% vs 82%; answer in first sentences 76% vs 71% (no real gap); definition format 60% vs 65% (no gap); hard number 6% vs 0%; novel claim 7% vs 0%.
  - Copilot cited passages that were more extractive (82% vs 70%) and fresher (89% vs 72%), with a weaker authority filter: two engines, two strategies.
- **AWR, "The Consensus-Information Gain Axis" (Apr 2026, Gianluca Fiorelli):** most-cited sources first align with consensus (proves relevance and trust) then add something new (justifies the slot). Cites Ahrefs (76.1% of AIO citations from top-10 pages in an older study, ~38% in a newer one, reconciled as "top-10 of the fan-out SERPs"), Search Engine Land (answer-first openings cited 67% more; original data tables 4.1x more citations). Not checked.

## The rule
Extractable, answer-first formatting gets your content USED. What gets you NAMED is the part the model cannot say without you: a dated claim, a hard number, a named case, a primary record, a disagreement. Pure consensus paragraphs are absorbed as common knowledge.

## How-to (per page, ~20 min)
1. **Consensus block (2-3 sentences):** answer the question the standard way, entity named in the first words ("Ty Mawr is...", not "This farm...").
2. **Information-gain block, visibly marked:** at least one of: a number with its source, a dated primary record, an original table, a named first-hand case, a stated point where the usual account is wrong and why.
3. **Visible date** near the passage ("Updated 4 Nov 2026" or the record's date). Dateless passages lose credit to dated ones.
4. **Right entity, not the neighbour:** check the page defines the exact thing asked (a page about a nearby concept loses the citation however good it is).
5. **Score before/after** with a 7-item checklist: entity at first mention / visible date / consensus line / number / primary-record line / original table or list / novel claim. Aim 5+/7 on money pages.
6. **Check both engines:** Google AIO rewards trust + spread across formats; Bing/Copilot rewards fast, fresh, extractive answers.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- Cedwyn's edge is pure information gain: dated primary records (tithe maps, 1905 place-name source, court references) that no consensus page holds. Lead each answer-first block with one consensus sentence, then the dated record line with its catalogue ID and year.
- /wales/thousand-cuts/ and /nomenclature/: the "In short" proposals already list dated facts; add a visible "Updated" date and ensure each starts with the exact entity name ("Ty Mawr ('Great House')...").
- /ebooks/: each e-book blurb carries one hard number from inside the book (pages, records reproduced, earliest date) so an AI answer about the topic has a reason to name the e-book.
- Corroboration surfaces: our quote cards on Instagram/Facebook and Shorts on YouTube are the "independent surfaces" pattern; keep each tied to the same dated fact and page so they agree.
- Use 'not checked' in public text for anything not checked; banned words apply. Body edits are PROPOSALS via the onpage flywheel.
- Add the 7-item checklist score as a column in the weekly visibility sheet for the 10 most-visited content pages.
