# Flywheel: Social follows

**Cadence:** three short runs a day (about 10:00, 14:30 and 19:00 UK)  
**Goal prefix (duplicate check):** `IG FOLLOW`  
**Runner:** `the follow runner on the project box (a detached process; the turn stays free)` (on the project workspace)

## Purpose
Grow the project's Instagram account by following relevant heritage, Welsh, local-area and genealogy institutions and accounts, within the platform's limits.

## Inputs
- a curated target list (handle, category, priority, why, source)
- follower lists of rotating heritage seed accounts when fewer than 200 targets are pending

## Outputs
- follows, each confirmed after a reload
- one log row per attempt

## Metric
follows per day within the cap, with zero restriction notices

## Report rules
Only on a restriction (with the new cap) or a lost login.

Built to the [flywheel schema](SCHEMA.md).
