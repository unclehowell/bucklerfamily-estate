# Flywheel: Social engagement

**Cadence:** three runs a day  
**Goal prefix (duplicate check):** `SOCIAL ENGAGEMENT`  
**Runner:** `the social engagement runner on the project box` (on the project workspace)

## Purpose
Comment on relevant local heritage posts with a short verbatim passage from the estate's own heritage pages, and follow related channels, to grow followers.

## Inputs
- recent local heritage videos and posts
- the quote bank (an allow-list of heritage pages)

## Outputs
- at most one comment per platform per run
- follows and subscriptions
- a log row per action or skip

## Metric
followers gained per week; comments that stick

## Report rules
Nothing routine; a message only on a new block or a lost login.

Built to the [flywheel schema](SCHEMA.md).
