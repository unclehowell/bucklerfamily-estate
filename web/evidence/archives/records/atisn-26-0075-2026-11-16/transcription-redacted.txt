Our response
Part A
An Internal Review of the response ATISN 26-0010 is currently being undertaken by the
Welsh Government Departmental Records Officer. You will receive the outcome of this
review separately in due course.

Part B
1. In responses to further supplementary enquiries from you following the Internal
Review of ATISN 27021, Cadw officials stated in emails of 10 July, 29 July and 27
August that, beyond the two photographs previously released to you under ATISN
27021, Welsh Government, including Cadw, does not hold any record of the
destruction or transfer of the information relating to Cadw’s 1988 decision not to add
Great House Farm to the list of buildings of special architectural and historic
interest. As a result, we consider your requests under ATISN 27589 to be
substantially similar to those you previously made under ATISN 27021.
2. As previously stated in Cadw’s emails of 10 July, 29 July and 27 August under
ATISN 27021, no further information is held by Welsh Government on any point
contained in your request ATISN 27589.
3. Given that your requests under ATISN 27589 are considered to be substantially
similar to those you made under ATISN 27021, which was subject to an Internal
Review, we refer you to your right to complain to the Information Commissioner.
Further details of how to do so are provided below.
