What the letter records

  • Part A — ATISN 26-0010: the internal review is still being undertaken by the Welsh Government Departmental Records Officer; the outcome will follow separately.
  • Part B — ATISN 27589: Cadw maintains that the request was substantially similar to ATISN 27021 and says no further information is held. It refers the requester to the Information Commissioner. It does not separately answer the request for clarification of the internal-review route for ATISN 27589.
  • Part C — registered-file records: four later registered files are said to be retained permanently and still held by the Welsh Government. The letter separately says six files of potential interest relate to the Tir Gofal agri-environment scheme (2006–2012), were marked for destruction, and have now been put on hold.
  • Part C — other searches: the letter states that no information is held for the further categories listed in the request: property/deeds, wartime farm records (1936–1955), and compulsory-purchase / stopping-up / diversion orders (1950–1995).
  • Part D — preservation: the original 29 July 1988 photographs and associated labels are said to form part of Cadw’s permanent photographic collections. It confirms a hold on the six files but does not expressly confirm the full wider preservation request for every 1988 file remnant, index, disposal record, retention schedule and transfer list.

Numbering and scope cautions

  • Part C identifies the six Tir Gofal file references as having been supplied at ATISN 26-0009 Document 4; Part D calls them the six files in Document 1. The discrepancy is reproduced rather than silently resolved.
  • Part C prints “2.– 6.” before the no-information-held statement, although the request’s Part C lists items 1–4. The apparent numbering defect is preserved as written.
  • The four permanently held files are dated 1997–2004. Their existence does not establish that they contain Great House Farm records or that they are the missing 1988 listing file.
  • The six files placed on hold are described as Tir Gofal files from 2006–2012. The letter does not say that they concern this farm.
  • This letter does not provide the date or documentary authority for the reported disposal of the 1988 file CL72/381. It does not complete the ATISN 26-0010 internal review.
