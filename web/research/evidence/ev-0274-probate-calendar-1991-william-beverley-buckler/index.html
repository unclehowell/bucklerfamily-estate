What the calendar establishes

The screenshot confirms that the HMCTS Find a Will calendar display contains an entry under the surname BUCKLER for William Beverley Buckler, with the address recorded as 4 Albert Cres, Penarth, South Glamorgan. The entry states a death date of 29 April 1990, records administration at Llandaff on 11 July 1991, gives the value band as not exceeding £115,000, and displays reference 9151806686J.

The displayed calendar is an official index/calendar record. It is not the underlying grant document and it is not a death certificate. The grant itself has not been ordered or inspected.

Family identification and the address

Family identification supplied by the deceased’s son on 19 November 2026: the William Beverley Buckler named in this entry is his father, William (“Billy”) Buckler. This is the son’s direct family identification and is recorded as such; the record no longer treats the identity as merely a possible match.

The son also clarifies that 4 Albert Crescent was the address of his father’s sister (the son’s late aunt). It is not to be described as the father’s own address or as proof that he lived there. The calendar records the address alongside the name; the family testimony explains whose address it was.

Date discrepancy: still unresolved

Keep the dates and their provenance distinct. The HMCTS calendar states 29 April 1990. The son previously reported 29 May 1990 as his father's date of death. No separate family-chronology document or supporting source for 29 May has been identified in the catalogue. That date is therefore recorded only as the son's earlier report and remains without independent documentary verification. The discrepancy remains open.

The South Wales Echo snippet dated 2 June 1990, which says Billy died “suddenly this week”, is broadly compatible with a late-May death but does not establish the exact date. The full newspaper item and a death certificate or other civil registration record would be needed to resolve the discrepancy.

Limitations and next verification

  • This is a screen capture of an HMCTS calendar display, not the underlying grant or a certified copy.
  • The screenshot does not itself record the family relationship; the identification as Billy is separately attributed to his son’s direct family testimony.
  • The family identifies 4 Albert Crescent as the address of Billy’s sister, not Billy’s own address.
  • The calendar's 29 April date conflicts with the family-reported 29 May date; this record does not resolve that conflict.
  • The visible screenshot does not expose the full image URL or search query used to reach the calendar page.

Further primary checks would be the underlying Llandaff grant record and the death registration/certificate, principally to resolve the 29 April / 29 May date discrepancy and inspect the grant’s details. The identity is recorded as family-identified by his son; the outstanding primary-record checks concern the documentary details and date, not whether the son recognises his father.
